I am also preparing bilingual user guides, checking screenshots, steps, and terminology against the real interface. I follow each user workflow to see whether a reader can find the right entry point, complete an action, and understand the information on screen.

When a workflow is described in another language, the wording still needs to match the interface. Parts that are difficult to explain also prompt me to reconsider how easy the product is to understand. I carry those observations back into the ongoing work.
