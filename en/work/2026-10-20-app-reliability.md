I am working on reliability and performance across platforms, including Flutter data sync failures, React startup, and map interactions. I begin by tracing the conditions in which a problem occurs, then inspect the related data and state changes.

Performance work also needs to account for existing features. I am looking at how loading timing, recovery from failures, and critical actions interact. Through code inspection and hands-on use, I assess whether a change addresses the immediate problem and which user workflows it affects.
